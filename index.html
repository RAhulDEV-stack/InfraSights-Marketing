Operational Reality

The SAN Visibility Challenge

Fibre Channel fabrics are mission-critical, yet diagnosing health and congestion often requires manually piecing together fragmented switch CLIs and disconnected screens.

Without Infrasights (Fragmented Ops)

  • Device-by-Device CLI Inspection: Engineers manually SSH into individual switches to parse porterrshow and show interface counters.
  • Hidden Buffer Credit Starvation: Slow-drain devices deplete buffer credits quietly, cascading across ISLs before alarms trigger.
  • Heavy Middleware Overhead: Relying on multi-tier management applications introduces licensing costs, delays, and software complexity.
  • Knowledge Concentration: SAN troubleshooting depends heavily on a handful of senior specialists who know where to look.

With Infrasights v2.10 (Operational Clarity)

  • Centralized SAN Visibility: Unified health, status, and port metrics across all fabrics in a single operational dashboard.
  • Direct Switch Data Collection: Direct API and telemetry ingestion straight from Fibre Channel switches without proprietary intermediaries.
  • Deterministic Congestion Diagnostics: Immediate tracking of zero-credit transitions, optical dBm degradation, and CRC bit errors.
  • Accessible SAN Operations: Empower the broader infrastructure and data center team to understand and triage fabric status quickly.
Platform Capabilities

Engineered for Fibre Channel Storage Operations

Infrasights v2.10 focuses on verified, deterministic hardware telemetry collected straight from director and edge switches.

Direct Switch Data Collection

Connects directly to SAN switches via secure REST APIs, SNMP, and SSH. Bypasses intermediate management software, eliminating extra points of failure and licensing overhead.

Zero Intermediary Dependency

Buffer Credit Starvation Tracking

Continuously monitors buffer-to-buffer (B2B) credit timers and zero-credit states. Instantly isolates slow-drain devices before backpressure degrades ISL trunks and stalls tier-0 storage.

Deterministic B2B Monitoring

SFP Physical Layer & Optics Diagnostics

Tracks Digital Optical Monitoring (DOM) metrics including TX/RX optical power (dBm), laser bias current, temperature, and supply voltage to detect degrading transceivers and cables prior to link flap.

DOM Transceiver Metrics

Hardware Error Register Correlation

Aggregates raw hardware counters—CRC errors, bit error rates (BER), encoding-out errors, and Class 3 discards—into prioritized fabric health indicators so you can distinguish dirty fibers from oversubscription.

Register-Level Accuracy

Fabric Topology & WWPN Zoning Audit

Provides live visualization of active zones, WWPN logins (FLOGI/PLOGI), and multipath fabric paths. Understand exactly which targets and initiators share links and where congestion can spread.

Name Server Integration

Focused Operational Investigation

Streamline investigations with unified search across WWPNs, switch hostnames, port aliases, and fabrics. Move directly from a high-level status indicator to the specific offending port in seconds.

Fast Root-Cause Triage
